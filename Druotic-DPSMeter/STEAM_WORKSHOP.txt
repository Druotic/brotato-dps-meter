[h1]DPSMeter - Accurate Real-Time DPS Overlay[/h1]

This mod adds a non-intrusive HUD overlay that shows rolling damage and DPS per player during runs.

[h2]What’s accurate here?[/h2]

[list]
[*]No overkill: the overlay records the `actual_dmg` returned by the enemy/neutral `take_damage()` call (not the requested damage).
[*]Charmed-enemy damage included: damage dealt by charmed enemies is attributed to the player who charmed them.
[/list]

[h2]Overlay contents[/h2]

[list]
[*]Pie chart showing each player’s share of rolling window damage.
[*]Per-player rows showing rolling window damage and rolling DPS.
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]

Optional: if you have [b]Oudstand-ModOptions[/b] installed:
- Go to `Options → Mods → DPSMeter`
- Toggle overlay and choose opacity / rolling window size.

[h2]Steam Workshop[/h2]

Install via Workshop (or drop the unpacked mod folder into `mods-unpacked/` following ModLoader best practices).
