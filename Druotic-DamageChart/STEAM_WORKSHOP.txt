[h1]Damage Chart - Real-Time Damage Visualization[/h1]

This mod adds a non-intrusive HUD overlay that shows total damage and average/peak DPS per player.

[h2]What’s accurate here?[/h2]

[list]
[*]No overkill: the overlay records the actual damage dealth, excluding overkill damage.
[*]Charmed-enemy damage included: damage dealt by charmed enemies is attributed to the player who charmed them.
[/list]

[h2]Overlay contents[/h2]

[list]
[*]Pie chart showing each player’s share of wave damage.
[*]Per-player rows showing wave damage and average/peak DPS (`damage so far / seconds so far`).
[/list]

[h2]Configuration[/h2]

To install, just subscribe to this mod and it will be loaded on next game launch.

Optional: if you have [b]Oudstand-ModOptions[/b] installed:
[list]
[*]Go to [code]Options -> Mods -> Damage Chart[/code]
[*]Toggle [b]Hide Damage Chart (Solo)[/b] if you only want the chart in co-op.
[/list]
